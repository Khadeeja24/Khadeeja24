## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🕵️ Multimodal Fake Review Detection System *(In Progress)*
> Detecting e-commerce fraud by jointly analyzing review **text + product images**

- Fusing **BERT** (text) + **ResNet-50** (images) through a **late-fusion FFNN** architecture
- Detects inconsistencies between review content and product visuals as fake-review signals
- Exploring **cross-modal attention** and transformer fine-tuning on imbalanced real-world datasets

---

### 🧬 Parkinson's Disease Detection — Multi-Model ML Pipeline
> Early detection of Parkinson's using biomedical voice measurements

- Benchmarked **7 classifiers**: Logistic Regression, Naive Bayes, KNN, Random Forest, SVM, XGBoost, ANN
- Applied **SMOTE** oversampling to resolve class imbalance and improve minority-class recall
- 🏆 Top model (ANN): **97% Accuracy · 100% Precision · 97% Recall · F1: 0.98 · ROC-AUC: 0.98**
- Conducted systematic hyperparameter tuning with reproducible benchmark results

---

### 📱 Impact of Social Media on Student Mental Health — Statistical Study
> Quantifying the relationship between social media usage and mental health in university students

- Designed and executed a **stratified random sampling** survey of **200 students** at Women's College, AMU
- Built structured questionnaires capturing **10+ quantitative & qualitative variables** in Google Forms
- Performed **hypothesis testing** on proportions using **R**
- Key findings: **58.4%** reported negative academic impact · **53.7%** experienced loneliness · **56.7%** had mood/emotional changes
- Validated effects on sleep quality, focus, and emotional well-being through significance testing
